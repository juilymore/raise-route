FreightLoop is a B2B SaaS platform that gives logistics operators one live view of shipments, fleet and

optimisation, and carrier performance analytics. Operators use it to cut late deliveries, reduce empty
miles, and replace spreadsheets, phone calls and WhatsApp groups with a single dashboard. It integrates with
existing ERPs, GPS/telematics providers and transport management systems through APIs.
